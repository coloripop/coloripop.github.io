Tier Framework

Tier FrameworkCriteria · Paths · KPIs

Operationalizes the maturity-model research into 4 tiers aligned to Capmation's AI Integration Playbook (Assisted, Augmented, Agentic, Orchestrator). No design-specific maturity model exists in the literature reviewed — this framework adapts general AI/engineering maturity research onto design-specific criteria and KPIs.
TierCriteriaKPI metricsPath to next tier
1. Assisted Isolated individual use (ideation, drafting); no shared team practice; no defined tool/prompt ownership AI tool adoption rate (91% weekly is the 2026 industry benchmark); toolstack baseline AI champion identified · training time allocated · usage documented across the team
2. Augmented AI embedded in specific stages (prototyping, research synthesis); informal, repeatable practice; heavy human review still required Design velocity (idea → tested prototype); rework rate; tool stickiness (90-day retention) Prompt/tool governance established · design system made machine-readable (tokens/MCP) · explicit checkpoint model
3. Agentic Agents operate with defined autonomy within guardrails; governance active (naming, versioning, audit trail); checkpoints enforced and logged Automation rate (60%+ at maturity, adjacent domains); reversion/rejection rate; handoff friction; hallucination/error rate (<1% target) Workflows redesigned AI-first · measurable ROI to business outcomes · legal/compliance sign-off achieved
4. Orchestrator Designers orchestrate multi-agent systems ("Agent Captains"); workflows AI-first by design; continuous monitoring built in Throughput per dollar of AI spend; business-outcome correlation; governance maturity score Continuous monitoring & improvement loop — the sustaining state, not a further gate
⚠ KPI benchmarks are adapted from adjacent domains (engineering, customer-service AI agents) — none are design-industry-specific. Treat as directional starting points, not proven benchmarks. Only 21% of organizations report a mature governance model for agentic AI even where 74% plan to deploy it within two years (Deloitte 2026, via Rohit Prabhakar) — Tier 4 is uncommon in practice across all domains, not just design.
