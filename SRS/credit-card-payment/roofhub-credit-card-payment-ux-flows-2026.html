⚡ Shop Pay (Shopify) — Accelerated Checkout Flow

⚡ Apple Pay — Biometric Checkout Flow

Apple Inc. · Best-in-class pattern · not a direct competitor · 2024–2026 sources
⚡ Best-in-class pattern — chosen for checkout speed, not because it competes with Roof Hub
Pattern to borrow: biometric confirmation replaces both the card form and the CVV field in one step — full card number is never seen by the merchant or typed by the user.
📱 APPLE PAY — PAYMENT SHEET
Strengths
  • ✓ Entire flow — button tap to authorization — typically completes in under 5 seconds.
  • ✓ Merchant never receives the real card number: a dynamic cryptogram + device token + dynamic CVV are sent instead.
  • ✓ Liability shift applies automatically — no additional issuer authentication step needed on top of biometrics.
Documented Friction Points
  • ⚠ Face ID can fail in public settings (masks, glare, awkward angles), forcing a passcode fallback — a documented UX case-study finding, not a hypothetical.
  • ⚠ Users often don't discover Apple Pay is supported until the final checkout step, since the button isn't shown earlier in the journey.

⚡ Stripe Link & Click to Pay — Network Tokenization Flow

Stripe · Visa/Mastercard EMVCo Click to Pay · Best-in-class pattern · not a direct competitor · 2026 sources
⚡ Best-in-class pattern — chosen for checkout speed, not because it competes with Roof Hub
Pattern to borrow: the card network itself (not just the merchant) remembers the device — so a shopper who has never visited Roof Hub before can still skip manual entry if they've enabled Click to Pay anywhere.
🖥 / 📱 LINK & CLICK TO PAY
Strengths
  • ✓ Stripe reports Link checkout is 3x faster than non-Link checkout, and lifts returning-user conversion by ~14%.
  • ✓ Click to Pay tokens auto-update if the physical card is reissued — no "update my expired card" dead-end.
  • ✓ Mastercard is rolling out payment passkeys as a password-free alternative to one-time codes, aiming to phase out manual card entry online by 2030.
Constraints
  • ⚠ First-time / unrecognized-device checkout still requires a one-time code step before autofill applies.

✦ Suggested Ideal Flow — Credit Card Payment

Synthesized from competitor & reference research (S3) · compared against Roof Hub's current design/prototype
✦ SUGGESTED IDEAL FLOW
CURRENT FLOW — ROOF HUB

Why this flow

  • Express/accelerated options (saved card, Apple Pay/Google Pay) are surfaced before any manual form — the single highest-leverage change benchmarked. (Based on: Shop Pay 1.72x conversion lift, Stripe Link 3x faster checkout — S3/S6) — [New]
  • Returning/express users confirm with a biometric scan or a single tap instead of re-entering anything. (Based on: Apple Pay payment sheet, Shop Pay one-tap pay — S3/S6) — [New]
  • New-card entry gets an optional camera-scan shortcut plus real-time Luhn validation and auto-formatted spacing — 53% of sites skip Luhn validation and 51% skip auto-format, both documented causes of failed payments. (Based on: Walmart camera-scan, L.L. Bean case study, Baymard Institute — S5/S6) — [New]
  • PAN is masked after entry (max first 6 + last 4 digits visible), satisfying PCI DSS 3.4.1. (Based on: PCI Security Standards Council — S8) — [New]
  • Save-card option issues a token with CVV-only re-entry on future orders, rather than storing/re-displaying the full card — the same principle behind Amazon's token and Stripe/Click to Pay's network token. (Based on: Amazon Payment Services, Stripe Link, Visa/Mastercard Click to Pay — S3/S6) — [Opportunity — no direct competitor documents this; best-in-class references are the closest evidence]
  • A visible processing/loading state sits between submission and confirmation. (Based on: Stripe checkout UX guide — universal friction point, S5) — [New]
  • Declined-payment screen names the likely reason and offers retry or an alternate payment method, instead of a generic error — the same pattern Amazon uses instead of a dead-end. (Based on: Amazon payment-issue handling, Baymard Institute "Card Declined" recovery research, Checkout.com retry guide — S5/S6) — [New]
Current Flow column reflects Roof Hub mockups shared in this session (Order Review, Homeowner Insights).
